Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

1.Who we are

Emirates Business Club (“EBC”, “we”, “us”) is a private business network based at 1006 International Business Tower, Business Bay, Dubai, UAE. We are responsible for the personal data collected through this website.

2.Information we collect

  • Details you submit in the registration form: full name, email address, WhatsApp number, company (optional) and professional role.
  • The content of messages you send us on WhatsApp.
  • Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and device, recorded in standard server logs.

3.How we use your information

  • To contact you about your founding membership and confirm your spot.
  • To tell you about the app launch, events, conferences and webinars.

4.Consent

We process your registration details based on your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw consent at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp. Withdrawing does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

5.WhatsApp

If you choose to message our concierge, the conversation takes place on WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta. Meta’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that service.

6.Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted service providers that help us run the website, process form submissions, host data or send communications, and with authorities where the law requires. Providers are only allowed to use your data for the services they perform for us.

7.International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the UAE. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data is protected to a standard consistent with applicable UAE data protection law.

8.How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as needed for the purposes above, or until you ask us to delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

9.Your rights

Under UAE data protection law, including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021, you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, object to its processing, or receive a copy of it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

10.Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11.Cookies and third-party content

This website does not currently use advertising cookies. Fonts and some images load from third-party servers, which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy first.

12.Children

The club is for business professionals aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

13.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
